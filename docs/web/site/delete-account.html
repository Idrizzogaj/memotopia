Delete your Memotopia account

App: Memotopia: Brain Training · Developer: Zogaj of Sweden AB. The steps are the same on iPhone and Android.

Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

1Open the side menu

Open Memotopia, log in and tap the menu button in the top corner.

2Tap Account

You find your profile and settings here.

3Tap Delete account

It is at the bottom. Enter your password (leave it empty if you signed in with Apple) and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and you are logged out.

Option 2 – by e-mail

If you cannot use the app, e-mail memotopia@memotopia.com from the address you registered with. Put "Delete my Memotopia account" in the subject and include your username. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address, username, avatar and password hash
  • Your progress: levels, stars, XP, achievements and statistics
  • Your favourites, and your place in challenges (other players keep their own results)

Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days until the backups are overwritten. We keep no copy for any other purpose.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).
