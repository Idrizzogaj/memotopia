Memotopia is a brain-training app with memory games (Pairs, Flash, Boxes, Chain), levels, achievements, a ranking and challenges between players. The app is for people aged 13 and over.

4. What other players can see

Your username, avatar, level, XP, achievements and aggregate challenge statistics are visible to other players, for example in search, in the ranking and when you are challenged. Detailed live progress and round results are shared with the participants in that challenge. Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

Online visibility has two audiences. Visibility to other players is off by default. When you send or accept a friend request, the app explains that accepted friends can see when you are online by default. You can switch off visibility to friends separately. Favourites are not friends. Presence is sent only while the signed-in app is active in the foreground; there is no location or background tracking or separate last-seen history. Status can take up to 45 seconds to disappear after a lost connection. The green/red online indicator represents visibility in Memotopia, not the device's Wi-Fi connection. A hidden player can appear offline; we do not reveal a hidden online state.

Only participants see an invitation and its decision. Declining adds no played game, loss or XP change. Your declined total and block list are private. Blocking stops invitations and presence visibility in both directions, removes friendship and closes pending invitations without a result. Unblocking does not restore friendship. Accepted game results can remain in game history.

5. Who receives data

  • Hetzner Online GmbH – server hosting and database, located in Germany (EU).
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) – sends confirmation and password-reset e-mails (France).
  • Apple and Google – subscription payments and, for Apple, "Sign in with Apple". We never receive your card details.
  • Unity Technologies – crash reports from the app. Unity may process this data outside the EU/EEA; such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Apart from the crash reports above, your data is stored within the EU/EEA.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: until you delete the account.
  • Presence expires within 45 seconds without a heartbeat. Expired lease values are cleared on the next challenge refresh; no separate activity history is kept.
  • Unaccepted friend requests, including refusals, expire after 7 days and are cleared on the next challenge refresh. Anti-spam counters keep the current or last one-hour window until the next request or account deletion.
  • Unanswered challenge invitations expire after 24 hours. Declined, expired, blocked and withdrawn challenge invitations are removed after 30 days on the next challenge refresh. Accepted game results and the private declined total remain with your account.
  • Friendships and privacy settings remain until changed or account deletion; block lists remain until unblocking or account deletion.
  • Unconfirmed registrations: deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Backups: a deleted account can remain in backups for up to 30 days until they are overwritten.
  • Payment records are kept by Apple and Google. Accounting records we must keep by law are kept as the law requires.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. Your account, progress, achievements, statistics, favourites, friendships, invitation records, presence settings and block relationships are then permanently removed. You can also ask us by e-mail; see how to delete your account. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to receive your data in a portable format and to withdraw consent. Write to memotopia@memotopia.com; we answer within one month. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. Confirmation links are single-use, expire after 24 hours and are stored only as hashes. We limit how often registration, login and reset requests can be made. No system is completely secure.

10. Children

Memotopia is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it. If local law requires a higher age or parental consent, that requirement also applies.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. Material changes are announced in the app or by e-mail.
