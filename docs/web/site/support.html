Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to memotopia@memotopia.com. Tell us your device, the app version (side menu) and what happened. We aim to answer within a few working days.

Common questions

I did not get the confirmation e-mail

Check your spam folder. The link is valid for 24 hours. If it has expired, register again with the same address.

I forgot my password

Use "Forgot password" on the login screen and follow the e-mail.

How do I cancel my subscription?

App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the account does not cancel it.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account.
