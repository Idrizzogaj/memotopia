Delete your Memotopia account

This page explains how to delete your Memotopia account (app: Memotopia: Brain Training, developer: Zogaj of Sweden AB).

Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Memotopia and log in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, enter your password (leave it empty if you signed in with Apple) and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and you are logged out.

Option 2 – by e-mail

If you cannot use the app, e-mail memotopia@memotopia.com from the address you registered with. Put "Delete my Memotopia account" in the subject and include your username. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete the account within [30] days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address, username, avatar and password hash
  • Your progress: levels, stars, XP, achievements and statistics
  • Your favourites, and your place in challenges (other players keep their own results)

Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days until the backups are overwritten. We do not keep any copy for other purposes.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) account.
