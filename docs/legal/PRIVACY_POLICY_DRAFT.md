## 2. What the app is
Memotopia is a brain-training app with memory games (Pairs, Flash, Boxes), levels, achievements, a ranking, and challenges between players. The app is for people aged **13 and over**.

## 4. What other players can see
Your **username, avatar, level, XP, achievements and challenge results** are visible to other players (for example in search, ranking and when you are challenged). Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

We use service providers that process data on our behalf under data-processing agreements:
- **Hetzner Online GmbH** – server hosting and database. The server is located in **Germany** (EU).
- **Brevo (Sendinblue SAS)** – sending account confirmation and password-reset e-mails (France).
- **Apple** and **Google** – payments for subscriptions and, for Apple, "Sign in with Apple". We do not receive your card details; Apple and Google process them under their own terms.

## 6. How long we keep data
- Your account and game data: until you delete the account.
- **Unconfirmed registrations** are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
- Server logs: **30 days** (proposed; the server must be set up to really delete older logs).
- Backups: deleted accounts can remain in backups for up to **30 days** (proposed) until they are overwritten.
- Payment records are kept by Apple/Google. If we must keep accounting records we keep them as the law requires.

## 7. Deleting your account
You can delete your account yourself **in the app: Account → "Delete account"**. Your account, progress, achievements, statistics and favourites are then permanently removed, and your place in challenges is removed (other players keep their own results). You can also ask us to delete it by e-mail to **memotopia@memotopia.com**; see https://memotopia.com/delete-account.html [[URL once published]].

**Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription.** Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

## 8. Your rights
You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, deleted, restricted, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to receive your data in a portable format, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Contact **memotopia@memotopia.com**; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**, www.imy.se.

## 9. Security
Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. E-mail confirmation links are single-use, expire after 24 hours, and are stored only as hashes. We limit how often registration, login and reset requests can be made. No system is completely secure.

## 10. Children
Memotopia is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it. [[Check the age-of-consent rule for each country you distribute to.]]

## 11. Changes
We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. Material changes are announced in the app or by e-mail.
