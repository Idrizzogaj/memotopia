# Saga synapse gate asset

Final project asset: `frontend/Assets/Resources/Saga/SynapseGate.png`.
Generated with the built-in image tool, then edited with that same tool at Idriz's request. No external API/CLI or paid-credit workflow was used. Existing neuron/map artwork was retained. The new image is a separate transparent sprite; runtime code supplies its electrical discharge. Import cap is1024; it is not CPU-readable. The central gap and exterior alpha are retained.

## Initial generation prompt

Create a single polished 2D game UI sprite for Memotopia Saga: a SYNAPSE GATE, two opposing axon terminal ends separated by a clearly visible transparent synaptic gap. Transparent background. Horizontal composition, 3:1 aspect ratio. Only TWO organic rounded bulbous turquoise/cyan nerve terminals facing one another: the left axon comes in from the left edge and ends at x=43%, the right axon enters from the right edge and ends at x=57%, leaving 14% empty transparent space in the middle. Both look like glossy softly hand-painted organic game illustrations, simple readable silhouettes at tiny mobile map sizes. Golden yellow MYELIN segments wrap each incoming long narrow axon towards the left and right edges, not around the facing terminal bulb tips. Rich turquoise membranes, subtle violet inner highlights, warm gold segmented sleeves; match friendly glossy neurons on a pastel lavender memory-game map. No complete neuron cell bodies or branching trees, no human objects, no literal fence, no padlock, no text, no numerals, no icons, no baked electrical arcs and no external glow. Keep all pixels outside the two terminals and their incoming axons fully transparent, including the central gap. Center vertically, both opposing ends clearly separated and symmetrical in visual weight. This sprite will be animated by code sending a signal across the gap.

## Final edit prompt

Reference/edit target: the preceding generated sprite. `transparent_background=true`.

Edit only the attached synapse-gate sprite. Preserve its exact glossy turquoise/violet and gold hand-painted game-art style, the transparent background, canvas size, left and right golden myelin sleeves and incoming axon shafts. Make BOTH facing bulbous terminal HEADS about 15 percent larger (slightly taller and fuller), and bring their faces a little closer together: reduce the clear central transparent gap by roughly one third, keeping a distinct narrow gap, without letting them touch or overlap. The change should be subtle, not a redesign. Keep the outer shafts and gold segments in their current places, smoothly connect the slightly larger heads to them. Keep the image symmetrical in visual weight. No lightning, no extra glow outside shapes, no text, no symbols, no background. Preserve all alpha transparency including the central synaptic gap.
